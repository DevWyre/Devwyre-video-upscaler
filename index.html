Devwyre's Video Upscaler What actually happens behind the scenes

Our Video Upscaler doesn't just stretch your video — that would only make it blurrier. A super-resolution neural network, trained on millions of images, looks at each frame and rebuilds the detail that isn't there: sharper edges, cleaner textures and less visible noise.

The process, step by step:

  1. 1 Demux. Your video's audio and picture tracks are separated. The audio is never touched — it's copied and re-attached at the end, so speech, music and timing stay exactly as they were.
  2. 2 Decode. The picture track is read frame by frame so every still image can be processed individually.
  3. 3 Reconstruct. Each frame runs through the AI model on your own graphics card (WebGPU), doubling the resolution and rebuilding lost detail. Models range from Fast to Ultra — more compute, even sharper results.
  4. 4 Preview. A before/after slider compares the original next to the upscaled version, and the scrubber lets you inspect any moment before you commit — without re-processing the whole video.
  5. 5 Encode & attach. The enhanced frames are compressed into a fresh MP4 (H.264) at your chosen resolution, then your original audio track is muxed back in — ready to download or save.

Everything runs locally, so your file never leaves your device and no server ever sees it. Longer videos take longer, but you can pause, cancel or re-preview any moment along the way.
